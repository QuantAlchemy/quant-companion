# Quant Companion AI summary

Canonical site: https://www.quant-companion.quantalchemy.io
Publisher: Quant Alchemy, https://quantalchemy.io

Quant Companion is a web application for strategy analysis, trade journaling,
and position sizing. Public tools process TradingView strategy exports in the
browser. The cloud journal and live market-data features require an approved
account. The product is educational software and does not provide investment
advice.
